## A record for an agent started by hand

**`bowery adopt <name> --launcher CMD --gated|--ungated`** gives a live agent
that was started by hand, with no dispatch record, one that `bowery resume`
can use: its session from herdr, its folder from its pane, and the launcher
and permission mode you give. Nothing restarts. That's how a seat started in
its pane can come under a job that resumes it later.

- **`--gated` or `--ungated` is required** for a new record of a claude
  agent: bowery can't see how a hand-started claude was launched, and resume
  would otherwise guess its permissions.
- **On an existing record,** `--launcher` sets or changes its launcher, and
  `--gated`/`--ungated` its permission mode.
- **Worktree paths are now optional;** `adopt <name> <path>...` still adds
  worktrees as before.
- `adopt` takes the same name lock as `resume` and `dispatch`.

## Upgrading

`bowery upgrade`, or the install line in the README.
