## What's new: a launch that fails says so on the bus

- **`office pull` publishes `job.failed`** when a job's surface exits
  non-zero, or can't be run at all (no command for it, a timeout). Before,
  the office's log got a `launched … exit N` line and the job was gone: no
  running job, no retry, no event, so nobody following the bus ever heard
  of it.
- The event comes from the office's own seat (in root mode, the socket's
  peer: the office's user). Its subject is `{job, office, kind}`; its data
  is `{surface, exit, stderr, why}`: `stderr` is the last non-empty line
  the surface wrote there (at most 500 bytes), `why` the error for a
  surface that never ran.
- The office's log records the outcome: `failed-published` with the
  record's `ref`, or `failed-not-published` with why (no bus node, or a
  seat the region's `[bus.seats]` doesn't let publish `job.failed`). A
  publish that fails never stops the pull, and waits at most 5 seconds on
  the node.
- To turn it on, give each office's user a seat in the region file:

  ```toml
  [bus.seats.<office user>]
  publish = ["job.failed"]
  ```

  and have whoever watches jobs follow `job.failed` (or `job.*`).

## Changed

- A surface's stderr goes to an unlinked temporary file instead of
  `/dev/null`, so its last line can be read back. A file, not a pipe: a
  process the surface leaves running can't hold up its exit.
