## What's new: the private projection, served to its owner's browser

- **`[bus.web]` in the region file** makes `office bus serve` also serve
  HTTPS on one tailnet address, for one tailnet login. A board on another
  origin reads the region's private records straight from the node, and
  writes them there, so nothing private passes through the board's
  server:
  - `GET /private/snapshot`: the private fold (`office private ls
    --json`'s shape), its cursor the node's heads, and a CSRF token;
  - `GET /private/stream`: server-sent events, the snapshot (or `ready`
    for a reader whose `?cursor=` or `Last-Event-ID` is current), then a
    `change` per change, `{cursor, put, drop}`, woken by the publish
    itself;
  - `POST /private/publish`: `{kind, data}`, a private topic only,
    checked by `bus.CheckPrivate`, published as the configured seat.
- **Who's asking is settled first.** The Host must be the node's own name
  (421 otherwise), an `Origin` must be the board's exactly (and a write
  must send one), and tailscaled's whois must say the caller is the owner
  on an untagged device: another login, a peer region's node or a server
  gets 403. A write carries the node's CSRF token for that login. CORS
  answers the board's origin alone, never `*`, with no credentials.
- **The certificate is tailscaled's** for the node's tailnet name, over
  its LocalAPI (root, the operator, or `TS_PERMIT_CERT_UID` for the
  node's user), cached beside the log and renewed a month before its end.
- `bus.WebHandler`, `bus.TailscaleWhoIs` and `bus.NewTailscaleCert` are
  importable, for a program that runs its own node.

### Fixed

- `scripts/test-suite` reads each shard's verdict from the line after its
  last `Ran N tests`, not from the log's last three lines. A test's own
  stdout is flushed when the shard exits, after the verdict, so two
  summary lines in one shard made a passing shard read as failed
  (`test_test_suite.py`).
